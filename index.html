Craftworks Home Watch

Know your place is fine, even when you are not there

Built for camps and second homes that sit empty for weeks or months. Sensors watch around the clock, and a real person walks through on a set schedule.

Set up the sensor kit

Leak, temperature, and power-loss sensors go in at the spots that cause the most trouble. A local hub keeps the data on site, with backup alerts if the internet drops.

Regular walk-throughs

Matthew walks the place on your plan's schedule and checks the things a sensor cannot see.

Photo report every visit

You get a short note with photos after each visit. Standard and Premium plans include a spring and fall exterior check with a written report. Drone reports are pending FAA Part 107 certification.

Fix what turns up

If a visit finds a loose railing or a worn step, we can quote the repair. You decide.

Pricing is subject to change, and final terms are set in your written contract. Drone reports are pending FAA Part 107 certification; until then, exterior checks are done from the ground. Repairs found on visits are billed separately at $65 an hour plus materials.

Home watch is a check-up service, not insurance. It doesn't insure your property, and lightning, floods, fires and other events can still happen. The goal is to catch leaks and problems early, before they get worse, but no inspection can catch everything. All work is done under a written contract that spells out what is and isn't included. No warranties or guarantees are made, and Adirondack Craftworks is not liable for damage to the property. Keep your own homeowners insurance in place.
